Traditional Art

With these two pieces I wanted to challenge myself to create something I hadn't before. Neither of these pieces has a name yet. This first piece I wanted to utilize only one pair of complements for color, exploring the neutrals they create together. The second piece is an attempt at impressionist style painting. Acrylic paint was used for all of these pieces.

I struggled with impressionist style painting and it's a rough spot I hope to work out. Technique is everything!
