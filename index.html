Hello! I’m Brittney Kuykendall, a graphic designer and bookkeeper helping business owners with everything from marketing and rebranding, to getting your books in shape! I had a focus on traditional art in my roots. And this work has lent me a great foundation for the importance of the details and diligence. Each client I work with is met with compassion and honesty. I strive to work closely with each one to fully understand their needs and their vision for their business and its future. Working together, I tailor my approach to suit your business needs, turning confusion into certainty. We will work together to ensure your brand reflects your intentions and the roll out of marketing campaigns is strategic and mindful. I offer monthly and catch-up bookkeeping services, expense tracking, and QuickBooks clean up to keep your business in order, see monthly reports, and manage the health of your business so you can focus on what really matters- running it! Every project and client is approached with strategic research and thinking, with a creative direction tailored to your goals and audience. Resulting in a brand that looks wonderful and easily connects, converts, and leads with a purpose. At Stone & Quill Studio we take your dreams and make them reality with our unique approach to meeting your business needs.
